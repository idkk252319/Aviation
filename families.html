Aircraft Families

Aircrafts belong to distinct families. The most prominant manufacturers are Airbus, Boeing, Embraer, ATR, and Bombardier.

The following selection will introduce you to the commercial families of the manufacturers Airbus & Boeing.

Airbus is of European origin. Many European countries take part in manufacturing various pieces of an Airbus Aircraft. Then they are assembled in Toulouse, France.
